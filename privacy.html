Privacy policy

Vivaah Planner is a student project that runs fully in your browser.

What we store

Your account details, wedding date, budget, guest names and phone numbers, tasks and vendor records are saved in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

Passwords

Because there is no server, passwords are kept in plain text in your browser. Do not reuse a password from another site.

Guest information

Only enter details of guests that you are allowed to share. You can delete any record at any time.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising.
